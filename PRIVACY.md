# Privacy Policy

Khidki is built so that message contents, attachments, and calls remain end-to-end encrypted between participants.

## Summary

- **Encrypted until opened**: Unread messages wait as AES-256-GCM ciphertext while you are offline and never start their timer on login until you open that specific chat.
- **1-minute post-seen cleanup**: Once seen, texts, photos, images, videos, and files clear automatically after **1 minute** (`60 seconds`) in both browser memory and cloud storage.
- **No phone number required**: Accounts use a verified personal or academic email address, a `3 to 10` character alphanumeric username, an optional image-only DP, and a password.
- **Logout vs Delete Account**:
  - **Logging out** clears active decrypted state on your device while preserving your account, username, DP, and encrypted key vault in cloud storage.
  - **Deleting your account** permanently erases your `auth.users` login, profile, DP, key vault, wrapped conversation keys, sent messages, 1-on-1 conversations, and call signals from cloud storage and wipes local device keys.
